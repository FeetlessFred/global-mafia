⚡ Quick Rules

☀️ Day: everyone alive can talk and vote.

🌙 Night: roles act during their own phase.

🗳️ You get one daytime vote.

💀 Dead players cannot chat.

📖 How to Play Global Mafia

Global Mafia is a social deduction game. The Mafia secretly try to eliminate the other players, while the Citizens try to discover who the Mafia are.

🎯 The Goal

🔴 Mafia: Eliminate enough non-Mafia players to take control of the game.

🟢 Citizens: Find and vote out the Mafia.

☀️ Daytime

Everyone who is alive can talk in the public chat. Discuss suspicious behaviour and try to work out who is Mafia.

During voting, tap a player's name. A confirmation box will appear. You must press Yes, Vote to submit your vote.

You can only vote once.

🌙 Nighttime

Night actions happen in separate phases.

  • 🔴 Mafia choose who they want to eliminate.
  • 🩺 Doctor chooses someone to protect.
  • 🔎 Detective investigates another player.

Night communication is private to the role currently acting.

💀 If You Die

Dead players can watch the game, but cannot participate in the chat or influence the game.

🏆 Winning

The game automatically checks the remaining players and determines whether the Mafia or Citizens have won.

💡 Tips

  • Pay attention to what people say.
  • Look at voting patterns.
  • Don't reveal your role without a reason.
  • The Mafia should avoid making themselves obvious.
  • Citizens should communicate and compare information.
  • Detectives should think carefully about when to reveal information.

🎭 Role Guide

🔴 Mafia

Work with the other Mafia players, choose targets during the Mafia phase, and survive the daytime votes.

🩺 Doctor

Choose a player to protect during the Doctor phase.

🔎 Detective

Investigate another player during the Detective phase to discover whether they are Mafia.

👤 Citizen

You have no night action. Use discussion, deduction and voting to find the Mafia.
