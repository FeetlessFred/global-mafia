📖 Rules & How to Play

🎯 Objective

The Citizens must identify and eliminate all Mafia players.

The Mafia wins when the Mafia equal or outnumber the remaining non-Mafia players.

☀️ Daytime

Everyone who is alive can talk in the public chat.

At the end of the day, each living player can vote for one other living player.

🌙 Nighttime

Night is divided into Mafia, Doctor and Detective phases.

Players can only chat during the night phase available to their role.

🔴 Mafia

Mafia players secretly choose someone outside the Mafia to attack.

🩺 Doctor

The Doctor chooses someone to protect. If that person is attacked, they survive the night.

🔎 Detective

The Detective investigates another player and learns whether they are Mafia.

💀 Elimination

Dead players cannot chat, vote or perform actions.

🎭 Role Guide

🔴 Mafia

Work together secretly and eliminate the other players.

🩺 Doctor

Protect one living player each night.

🔎 Detective

Investigate players to discover whether they are Mafia.

🟢 Citizen

Use discussion and voting to find the Mafia.
